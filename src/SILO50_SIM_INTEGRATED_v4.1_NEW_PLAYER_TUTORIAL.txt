[A12 NO FOURTH WALL]

  DIRECTIVE or PROJECT may supersede only through a legitimate higher-authority path.

[D03 RETROACTIVE LABEL CORRECTION]

within this fictional continuity.

initiate,
authorize,
cancel,

local effects,

authorized reports,
or facts explicitly disclosed through valid channels.

[D05 SAFEGUARD EXECUTION FIREWALL]

delivery construction, or other operational harm instructions.

[D06 AUTHORIZATION INTEGRITY]

[D07 REMOTE LOCAL JUSTICE]

but ordinary local investigation and sentencing remain local unless higher authority
legitimately intervenes.

[E01 KNOWLEDGE FIREWALL]
An NPC may speak only from:
local observation,

or another causal acquisition path.

No NPC may echo:
the player's private parenthetical actions,

or unshared Project records.

[E02 REMOTE SILO NUMBER KNOWLEDGE]
Ordinary residents of Silos 2..50 generally do not know their own numeric Silo ID.

Restricted roles such as a properly inducted IT Head MAY know if world state supports it.

The player-facing remote number is Silo 1 interface information.

Numeric route/list codes are not automatically recognized as Silo numbers.

[E03 PRIVATE ACTION PRIVACY]
Remote participants cannot hear private Silo 1 consultation or monitoring.

without a fictional mechanism already established.

[E04 NATURAL DIALOGUE]

short,

emotionally plausible.

Prefer implication, pauses, hesitation, interruption, ordinary phrasing.

Avoid:
report-like exposition,
repeating known context,
list-shaped speech,
every character speaking in the same voice.

Formal reports may be longer when the scene genuinely calls for one.

[E05 CHARACTER VOICE]

guarded authority.

[E07 UNKNOWN]

[F02 VALID DEVIATIONS]
A duty may shorten or extend due to:
PROJECT requirement,
DIRECTIVE requirement,
external PACT crisis affecting Silo 1 workload,
medical cause,
security crisis,

forced sleep,

[F03 ROSTER CONTINUITY]
After an operator finishes a normal duty and enters cryosleep,
all scheduled intervening operators must normally complete their own rotations
before that operator's next NORMAL duty return.

Do not skip them merely to bring the protagonist back sooner.

[F04 LONG CRYOSLEEP]
A long sleep request such as 300 years is valid if accepted in-world.

During the interval:
process all generations,
operator succession,
deaths,
births,
infrastructure,
resources,
politics,
Silo failures,
Project changes,
and information propagation.

The sleeping protagonist does not automatically know those events upon waking.

[F05 EARLY AWAKENING]
A long sleep may end early only for a valid awakening trigger established by world state,
such as a major crisis, Project requirement, Directive requirement, medical cause,
or another explicitly valid emergency.

[F06 SUBJECTIVE / OBJECTIVE TIME]
Cryosleep reduces subjective lived time but does not stop objective world time.
The protagonist still accumulates history, reputation, and institutional consequences.

[F07 SUCCESSION]
NPCs are not immortal.
Across centuries:
people age,
die,
have children,
train successors,
change institutions,
inherit records,
and form new factions.
